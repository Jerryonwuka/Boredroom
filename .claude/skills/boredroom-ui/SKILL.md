# Boredroom UI (design system v4)

Read `docs/design-system.md` first; it records the tokens, the accent rules, the parts and the reasons. The values live in `src/app/globals.css`; every part in every state, dark and light, is on `/dev/design` (development only). Then:

1. **Build from the primitives** in `src/components/ui/`: `Button`/`buttonVariants`, `IconButton`, `Input`/`Select`/`Textarea`/`Field`, `Switch`/`Checkbox`/`Radio`, `Segmented`, `Tabs`, `Badge`/`CountPill`/`NewBadge`, `Card`/`PageHeader`/`SectionTitle`/`Ledger`, `StatCard`, `AnalyticsCard`, `DataTable`, `ListRow`/`SubNavItem`, `Sheet`/`Dialog`, `ConfirmButton`, `Menu`, `EmptyState`/`Alert`/`Skeleton`, `ProgressBar`/`ProgressArc`, `Slider`, `StatusDot`/`LiveIndicator`, the charts, `ToolTile`/`ToolTileRow`, `PromptInputBox`, `DatePicker`. Never hand-roll buttons, dialogs, inputs, tabs, menus or cards. The logo is always `<Logo />`/`<LogoArt />` from `src/components/logo.tsx`; never draw or type it.
2. **Tokens only:** `bg-background`, `bg-sidebar`, `text-foreground`, `text-secondary`, `text-subtle`, `text-faint`, `border-border`, `border-border-input`, `bg-fill-0`/`fill-1`/`fill-150`, `bg-surface`, `bg-accent`, `text-accent-text`, the status colours, `shadow-natural-xs`/`chart`/`sheet`/`toast`, `var(--z-*)`. No raw hex, no arbitrary z-index, no gradients, glass, glow or aurora (one owner-approved exception: Brenda's home panel and orb glow, see "Brenda's home panel" in docs/design-system.md; use it nowhere else). The v3 names (`.tile`, `.chip`, `text-fg-muted`, `bg-wash`, `--bg` …) are deleted; do not bring them back.
3. **Type:** Inter 14/20 medium for the interface; the display face (Geist) only for page titles and the home headline (`.type-page-title`, `.type-headline`); `.type-section-title`, `.type-meta`, `.type-caption`, `.type-stat`, `.type-metric`; tabular numbers. Sentence case, no tracked capitals, no middle-dot meta lines (use commas).
4. **Orange follows the accent rules** (owner decision, 6 October 2026): it marks what is live, active, chosen or the one thing to do, never decoration. The active nav icon, the orange tab underline, attention counts (`CountPill tone="attention"`), at most ONE `variant="accent"` button per screen (every other primary stays white), running timers and live dots (`StatusDot tone="live"`, digits in `text-accent-text`), recording and Brenda listening, progress and the chart highlight, checked boxes, the chosen segmented dot, the 2px selected marker (`ListRow active`, `SubNavItem`, `.selected-marker`), `.link-inline` hover, "New" badges, the empty-state square. Status colours win over orange (green done, amber paused or warning, red danger). Budget: 3 to 6 small touches a screen; if busy, remove the least important. Orange text is `text-accent-text`; fills and icons are `bg-accent`/`text-accent`; text on orange is `text-accent-fg`.
5. **Layout:** the shell (256px sidebar, 50px top bar marked `data-app-topbar`, 20px page sides), `PageHeader` with its tabs, sheets from the right for forms and detail, centred dialogs for confirms. Keep the Brenda chat contract (`data-brenda-chat`, `#main`, `#workspace-sidebar`) as the docs describe.
6. **Motion:** colours 75ms, tabs and menus 150–200ms, sheets 300ms ease-out; nothing bounces; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns movement off.
7. **Accessibility:** an accessible name on every control (icon buttons need `aria-label`), `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` on toggles, errors linked to fields, keyboard reachable with the visible orange focus ring, dialogs on the native `<dialog>`, a word beside every colour (orange never carries meaning alone), 40px touch targets on coarse pointers, both themes. Destructive actions go through `ConfirmButton`, never `window.confirm`.
8. **Empty, loading, error:** empty states name one next action; loading uses skeletons; permission-denied pages still carry their h1.
9. **The desktop notch** (`desktop/src/style.css`) copies the dark tokens and follows the same accent rules; change a token in both places.
10. **Verify** at desktop width and at ~400px (no sideways scroll, actions not wrapping between a title and its description), in dark and light, before presenting. Then `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` and `npx eslint` on the files you touched.
